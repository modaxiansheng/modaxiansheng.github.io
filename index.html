Ziying Song 宋子盈

Research Fellow · Nanyang Technological University
Lecturer · Yanshan University

I am a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Chen Lv, and a Lecturer and Master's Supervisor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Yanshan University.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2026, advised by Prof. Caiyan Jia. During my doctoral studies, I also conducted joint research at Tsinghua University.

My research focuses on world models, embodied intelligence, and autonomous driving. I am interested in how predictive models and generative planning can turn multimodal observations into reliable actions in the physical world.
